# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and focused on
technical collaboration.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal
attacks, deliberate disruption, doxxing, or publishing private security details
outside the coordinated disclosure process.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or limit
participation when behavior harms the project or its contributors.

Security reports should follow SECURITY.md rather than public discussion.

## Contact

For conduct concerns, contact the project maintainers through the repository
maintainer channels or security@gateway.fm when privacy is required.
